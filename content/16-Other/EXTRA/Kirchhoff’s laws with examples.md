## Overview

> [!info] Kirchhoff gave **two fundamental laws** for analysing electrical circuits:
> 
> 1. **KCL** — Kirchhoff's Current Law (Junction Rule)
> 2. **KVL** — Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (Loop Rule)

---

## 1. Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)

> [!tip] Statement _The algebraic sum of currents entering or leaving the junction is **zero**._ $$\sum I = 0$$

- It is based on the **principle of conservation of charge**
- and Applicable to **linear elements only**

### Explanation

$$I_{in} = I_{out}$$

If currents $I_1$ and $I_2$ enter a node, and $I_3$ leaves:

$$I_1 + I_2 = I_3$$

### Sign Convention

|Current Direction|Sign|
|---|---|
|Entering the node|$+$ (Positive)|
|Leaving the node|$-$ (Negative)|

---

## 2. Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

> [!tip] Statement _The algebraic sum of all potential differences around any closed loop/mesh is **zero**._ $$\sum V = 0$$

- Also known as **Kirchhoff's Loop Rule**
- Based on the **principle of conservation of energy**

---

## Summary Table

| Law | Applies To      | Based On               | Formula      |
| --- | --------------- | ---------------------- | ------------ |
| KCL | Node / Junction | Conservation of Charge | $\sum I = 0$ |
| KVL | Loop / Mesh     | Conservation of Energy | $\sum V = 0$ |

---

## Quick Revision Callout

> [!summary] Key Points for Exam
> 
> - **KCL** → at a **node** → $\sum I = 0$
> - **KVL** → in a **loop** → $\sum V = 0$
> - KCL is valid for **linear elements only**
> - KVL is based on **conservation of energy**
> - KCL is based on **conservation of charge**
