## Definition
A **PMMC (Permanent Magnet Moving Coil) instrument** is used to measure **DC current and voltage**.

## Principle
It works on the principle that **a current carrying conductor placed in a magnetic field experiences a force**, which causes the **coil to rotate**.

## Working
When **DC current flows through the moving coil**, it interacts with the **magnetic field of the permanent magnet** and produces **deflecting torque**. This moves the **pointer over the scale**, indicating the value of current or voltage.

> **Note:** PMMC instruments measure **only DC**, because AC produces zero average torque.

## Main Parts
- Permanent magnet  
- Moving coil  
- Soft iron core  
- Pointer and scale
