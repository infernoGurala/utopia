# Electrical Machines & Energy Conversion

> [!abstract] Definition
> An **electrical machine** converts energy between **electrical ↔ mechanical** forms via **electromagnetic interaction**.

---
### THREE MACHINE TYPES

> [!motor] Electric Motor
> Converts **electrical energy → mechanical energy**
> 
> <span style="background:#9FE1CB;color:#085041;padding:2px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-size:12px">Electrical</span> → <span style="background:#E1F5EE;color:#085041;padding:2px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-size:12px">Mechanical</span>

> [!generator] Generator
> Converts **mechanical energy → electrical energy**
> 
> <span style="background:#E1F5EE;color:#085041;padding:2px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-size:12px">Mechanical</span> → <span style="background:#CECBF6;color:#26215C;padding:2px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-size:12px">Electrical</span>

> [!transformer] Transformer
> Transfers electrical energy between circuits — **frequency unchanged**
> 
> <span style="background:#FAC775;color:#412402;padding:2px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-size:12px">Circuit A</span> → <span style="background:#FAC775;color:#412402;padding:2px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-size:12px">Circuit B</span>

---
### ENERGY CONVERSION PRINCIPLE

> [!principle] Current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field → Force
> A conductor carrying current inside a magnetic field experiences a **mechanical force** ($F = BIL$).
> This force produces **motion** — the principle behind **electric motors**.

> [!principle] Moving conductor in a magnetic field → EMF
> When a conductor moves through a magnetic field, an **electromotive force (EMF)** is induced (Faraday's Law).
> This is how **generators** produce electricity.
