## AC vs DC Supply

|               | **AC — Alternating Current**    | **DC — Direct Current**       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Flow**      | Direction flips back and forth  | Always flows one direction    |
| **Source**    | Power grid, wall socket         | Batteries, solar panels, USB  |
| **Used in**   | Fans, AC units, home appliances | Phones, laptops, electronics  |
| **Key fact**  | Easy to transmit long distance  | Needed by all digital devices |
| **frequency** | Has a frequency of 50Hz or 60Hz | no frequency                  |
| **Voltage**   | varies with time                | constant                      |
