**Time Period (T)** — The time taken to complete one full cycle of a wave. Measured in seconds.

**Frequency (f)** — How many cycles happen in one second. Measured in Hz. It is simply the inverse of time period: f = 1/T.

**Amplitude** — The maximum displacement of the wave from its center (zero) line. It is the "height" of the wave from the middle.

**Peak to Peak Value** — The total distance from the highest point to the lowest point of the wave. It is simply 2 × Amplitude.
